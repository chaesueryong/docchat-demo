# Plans and Billing

Northwind Cloud offers three plans. You can change plans at any time from the dashboard under Settings → Billing.

## Plans

The **Free plan** costs $0 and includes 100 GB of bandwidth per month, 1 concurrent build, and community support. Projects on the Free plan sleep after 30 minutes of inactivity.

The **Pro plan** costs $20 per member per month. It includes 1 TB of bandwidth, 4 concurrent builds, custom domains with automatic SSL, and email support with a 24-hour response target. Pro projects never sleep.

The **Enterprise plan** has custom pricing. It adds SSO/SAML, audit logs, a 99.95% uptime SLA, dedicated support with a 1-hour response target, and invoice-based billing. Contact sales for a quote.

## Usage-based billing

Bandwidth beyond your plan's included amount is billed at $0.10 per GB at the end of each billing cycle. Build minutes beyond the included 6,000 minutes per month are billed at $0.01 per minute. You can set a hard spending cap under Settings → Billing → Spend Limit; when the cap is reached, deployments pause rather than incurring charges.

## Invoices and payment

Payments are processed on the 1st of each month via credit card. Enterprise customers can pay by invoice with NET-30 terms. All invoices are available for download as PDF under Settings → Billing → Invoices. We do not store card numbers on our servers; payment details are handled by our payment processor.

## Refunds

Monthly plans can be canceled anytime and remain active until the end of the billing period. We offer a full refund within 14 days of a first-time Pro subscription. Usage-based charges (bandwidth overages, build minutes) are not refundable.
